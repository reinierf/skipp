# Alpha-beta search benchmark (Original board)

Generated by `src/analysis/search-bench.ts`: 50 positions from data/branching-ab4/positions.json (10 per game phase), iterative deepening to depth 14 with a 10s cap per position, in 357.8s.
Depth is in plies; one ply is one complete turn (base step + top step).
Search: negamax alpha-beta with PVS, iterative deepening, transposition table, in-place make/unmake; ordering: TT move > captures > killers > history + advancement.
Evaluation: tops, squared advancement, immediate goal threats, top and base mobility.

Search speed: 1.2M nodes/s.
