# Alpha-beta search benchmark (Original board)

Generated by `src/analysis/search-bench.ts`: 50 positions from data/positions.json (10 per game phase), iterative deepening to depth 10 with a 10s cap per position, in 338.5s.
Depth is in plies; one ply is one complete turn (base step + top step).
Search: negamax alpha-beta, iterative deepening, ordering wins > captures > advancement, no transposition table.
Evaluation: tops, squared advancement, immediate goal threats, top and base mobility.

Search speed: 710.1k nodes/s.
