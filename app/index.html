How to play

Get one of your tops onto the dark grey row on the far side to win.

Each turn has two steps:

  1. Move a base: one of your bases without a top moves one cell straight forward, into an empty cell or swapping with an empty opponent base. This step is mandatory when possible.
  2. Move a top diagonally forward, only over bases. Sprint any distance over a straight run of empty bases, or jump an adjacent opponent top onto the empty base behind it, removing that top.

If neither player can move, the game is a draw. Full rules
